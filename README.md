# HOTFIX

**An interactive LitRPG comedy in four books, played in a single web page.**

You are a regular person who finds a file that controls reality. You can edit it. The catch: every edit has consequences, and the file is not well maintained. Your choices decide what happens, so no two runs play the same way.

HOTFIX is the **Write Access** series, a funny, fast, endlessly replayable story about changing the world one config line at a time.


## The four books

| Book | Title | What happens |
|------|-------|--------------|
| One | **Write Access** | You find the file and learn that Tuesdays can be edited. |
| Two | **Merge Request** | Your changes collide with everyone else's. A review is coming. |
| Three | **Dependency Hell** | Reality runs on packages, subscriptions and a very tall tower. |
| Four | **Final Release** | End of Support is three days away. Close five blockers, say your goodbyes, and meet the Author. |

Finishing a book carries your character forward into the next. Each book has its own endings, and the finale has nine.

## Features

- Hundreds of scenes with real branching: your choices change flags, relationships and what the world looks like
- Reality editing: rewrite lines of `reality.cfg` and watch the consequences play out, with side effects that echo later
- Stats, skills, levels (cap 25), checks, items, allies, and boss fights you can win by fighting, by talking, or by editing the rules
- Drift and Heat meters with their own fail states
- A large recurring cast, including a linter, a bus driver, a duck, and a change machine
- 39 endings across the series, hidden achievements, and a Records screen that tracks them
- A freeform "write the last line yourself" ending in each book
- Adjustable font size; dark terminal look, no external requests
