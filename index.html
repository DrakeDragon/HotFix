HOTFIX

Reality runs on a text file. You just found the file. Nobody told you it was editable, and nobody told the people whose job it is to protect it that you can read it. Every choice you make is a line of config, and every line has side effects.

reality.cfg
gravity.strength   = 9.8
tuesday.duration   = 24h
socks.loss_rate    = 0.30
# do not edit

Every run plays differently. Your edits come back to haunt you, help you, or form a union. Autosaves on this device.
